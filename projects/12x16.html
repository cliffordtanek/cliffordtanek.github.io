Level design · Residential · 2023 P-10

12 × 16

A 12m × 16m residence for an isolated, work-from-home young adult — every essential living function on a very small plot, designed in two weeks.

The brief

Fit sleeping, cooking, eating, sanitation and working onto a 12m × 16m plot, for someone who does all of it alone and without leaving.

The design

I wrote the occupant as a landscape architect working from home, which gave the layout a reason to have opinions. There are several workspaces at different levels of privacy rather than one desk, a garden reachable from both the bedroom and the living room, and windows positioned so that every workspace has both daylight and something worth looking at.
